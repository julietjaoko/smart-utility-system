How Billing Works
  • Utility rates define the cost per unit for water and electricity.
  • Fixed charges cover monthly fees such as garbage collection, security, and maintenance.
  • Invoice calculation: (Water units x Rate) + (Electricity units x Rate) + Fixed Charges.
  • Outstanding balances or credits are carried forward automatically.
